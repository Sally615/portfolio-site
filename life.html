01 — Move

In the water, on the court

A swimmer since nine

I started swimming at nine and went on to compete as a National Level 2 swimmer in China.

The funny part: all my athletic genes may have gone to swimming. I fell for tennis six months ago and my progress has been slow. If you’re in Seattle and want a patient hitting partner, come practice with me.

Between the two, there is yoga, and in winter, snow.

02 — Music

Four strings

Growing up with the pipa

I grew up playing the pipa, the Chinese lute, taught by a teacher and his wife from a family of artists in Nanjing. I later became principal pipa in my university orchestra.

My high school orchestra was well known. The bassist from my class went professional and is now a principal at the Shanghai Symphony Orchestra.

This May brought my fangirl moment: a concert by Wu Man, the most celebrated pipa player in the world, with that same classmate on bass.

And a lot of Taiwanese bands

In pop music, my heart belongs to bands from Taiwan. My playlist will be on at the Winter Prom. Come dance.

03 — Travel

Two hemispheres

Half a Southern Hemisphere citizen

My Shangri-La

I also hold residency in Australia, which makes me half a citizen of the Southern Hemisphere. The easygoing pace down there is a real part of who I am.

Quiet, restrained, exact

A long love for Japan

I have loved the restraint of Japanese aesthetics since I was a child: nothing extra, everything considered.

I once passed the JLPT N3. These days my Japanese has faded to exactly what I need to order dinner.
